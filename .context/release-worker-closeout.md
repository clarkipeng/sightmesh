# Final closeout

Perform a final read-only reconciliation of your assignment.

Report:

- objective and delivered artifacts;
- workspace and session IDs;
- branch, exact HEAD, base SHA, upstream, and draft PR;
- exact validation results;
- dirty, untracked, unpushed, or unmerged work;
- open feedback and current CI state;
- explicitly deferred scope and its owner;
- the next exact integration action;
- whether this clean managed worktree is safe to archive.

Do not change code, push another commit, mark the PR ready, merge, or expand scope. Stop after the factual closeout.
